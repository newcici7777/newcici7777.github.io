## I have got to = I have to (我必須)
- I have got to = I have to = I must 我必須
- 否定: I haven't got to + 原形動詞 (我不需要)
  - 原句: I don't have to + 原形動詞 (我不需要)
- 疑問句: Have you got to go? 我需要去嗎？
  - 疑問句要把have移到最前面。
  - 原句: Do I have to go? 我需要去嗎
  - ❌ 錯誤: Do I have **got** to go? 有got就要把Have移到最前面

  
例句:
- I've got to get dressed before I do anything else.在我做其他任何事情之前，我必須先穿好衣服。
- What have I got to do before my parents come?在我父母來之前，我必須做什麼？
  - 原句: What do I have to do before my parents come?
- Have I got to clean my room?（我非得打掃房間不可嗎？）

-------------------------

## I gotta = I have to (我必須/我得要...) 
got to（＝ have to / must，必須、我得要...）

```
主詞 + gotta + 原形動詞
```
- I gotta go now.（我現在得走了。）
- You gotta see this movie!（你一定要看看這部電影！）
- We gotta hurry.（我們得趕快。）
- Gotta go.（我得走了。）
- I gotta get dressed.（我得去穿衣服。）
- I gotta go to the bathroom right now!（我現在馬上得去上廁所！)

---------------------------
## I've got a = I have a (有)
- I've got a problem.我有個問題
  - 原句: I have a problem.
- Have you got a minute?你有空嗎/你有分鐘時間嗎
  - 原句: Do you have a minute?
  - 有have got問句，就要把Have移到最前面當助動詞。

## gotta = have a (有)
- I gotta problem.（我有個問題。＝ I have a problem.）
