## 享用 + 食物名詞
- Enjoy the pizza!
- Enjoy your coffee!
- Enjoy your meal.

## enjoy 享受 + 名詞
* We enjoyed our stay at the hotel.（我們很享受在飯店停留的這段時間。 → 名詞）
- We stopped at a nice café along the coast to enjoy the ocean view.（我們沿著海岸順道去了一家不錯的咖啡廳，欣賞海景。）
- We enjoyed the party very much. 我們非常享受這場派對

## enjoy（享受）+ Ving
你必須正在做、或做過某件事，才能享受它
- Many of my friends enjoy traveling.（我的許多朋友都喜歡旅行。）
- Kevin enjoyed his trip to New York last year.Kevin很享受他去年去纽约的旅行。
- Did you enjoy your trip to Chicago last summer? 你很享受你去Chicago的旅行吗？
- Kevin enjoyed his trip to New York last year.Kevin很享受他去年去纽约的旅行。
- I enjoy reading books. 我喜歡閱讀。  
- She enjoys playing tennis. 她喜歡打網球。  


## enjoy + 名詞 喜歡的事物
- I usually don't enjoy American food,but last night I really enjoyed it!

## enjoy + Ving 喜歡做某事
* *She enjoys reading **rather than watching** TV.*
（她喜歡閱讀，**而不是**看電視。）
