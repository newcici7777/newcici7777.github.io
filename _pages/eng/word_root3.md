- strict [strɪkt] adj.嚴格的；嚴厲的 [🔊 聽發音](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/strict)

  strict 拉緊 = 不寬鬆  
  例句1: The school has strict rules. 這所學校有嚴格的規定。  
  例句2: She is very strict with her children. 她對孩子非常嚴格。  
  例句3: There are strict regulations on food safety. 食品安全有嚴格的規範。  

- strict**ly** [ˈstrɪktli] adv.嚴格地 [🔊 聽發音](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/strictly)

  strict 嚴格的 ly 副詞  
  例句1: Smoking is strictly forbidden. 嚴格禁止吸煙。  
  例句2: Please follow the instructions strictly. 請嚴格遵守指示。  
  例句3: The data is strictly confidential. 這些資料是嚴格保密的。  

- **re**strict [rɪˈstrɪkt] v.限制 [🔊 聽發音](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/restrict)

  re 反覆 strict 拉緊（反覆拉緊 → 限制）  
  例句1: The new law restricts the sale of alcohol. 新法律限制了酒的銷售。  
  例句2: He restricts himself to two cups of coffee a day. 他限制自己一天只喝兩杯咖啡。  
  例句3: Access is restricted to authorized personnel. 僅限授權人員進入。  

- **re**strict**ion** [rɪˈstrɪkʃən] n.限制；限制規定或法規 [🔊 聽發音](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/restriction)

  re 反覆 strict 拉緊 ion 名詞  
  例句1: There are speed restrictions on this road. 這條路有速度限制。  
  例句2: The government imposed trade restrictions. 政府實施了貿易限制。  
  例句3: Diet restrictions are necessary for her health. 飲食限制對她的健康是必要的。
