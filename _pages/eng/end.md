## the end of this street 街道盡頭
- Go straight **to** the end of this street.直行到这条街的尽头。
- Go straight, and turn left **at** the end of this street.直行，然后在这条街的尽头左转。
- Turn left at the end of this street.
在这条街的尽头左转。

-----------------

## the end of the line (隊伍尾巴)
- Where is the end of the line? 隊伍的尾巴在哪裡？

------------------

## At the end of + 地點 (盡頭)
- At the end of the hall（在走廊的盡頭）
- Is there a restroom at the end of the hall ? 走廊盡頭有洗手間嗎？
- Is there a bookstore at the end of that
street?
- Her room is at the end of the hallway. 她的房間在走廊的盡頭。  

----------------

## at the end of 季節/月份 (季末/月底)
- Our kids will start school at the end of August.(我们的孩子们会在八月底开学。)
- The store discounts its products at the end of the season. 這家商店在季末打折。

-----

## at the end 最後(副)
- Keep your workspace clean as you go so you don't have a huge mess at the end. （你在動手做的同時順便保持工作區乾淨，這樣最後才不會一團亂。)

## in the end 最終
- You know she always saves the day in the end!你知道她最后总能挽救局面！

----------------------------

## the final + 名詞 (最後的)
* Let's welcome the final model, Lisa!讓我們歡迎最後一位模特，麗莎！
