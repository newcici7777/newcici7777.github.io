## buy
### 人 + buy + 人 + 物

- She'll buy her parents some beans.
- Can you buy us some milk?

### 人 + buy + 物

她下周会买一辆新车。  
She will buy a new car next week. 

### 人 + buy + 物 + for + 人

通過努力(力氣)幫某人做某事 都要用for 人

ex:buy 買、make 做、cook 煮、get 拿

- I need to buy lots of fruit for a party.
- He wants to buy some plants for his flower garden(他想要给他的花园买一些植物。)

### 人 + buy + 東西 + for + 錢
Can I buy it for one hundred dollars.

你可以在這裡為你的飲料付錢，注意！此時飲料為可數名詞加s。

l'm paying for the ride,

## send
### 人 + send + 人 + 物
- I want to send them some bananas from my farm.
- Grandpa will send us some new books.
- Can you send him an email?

## give
### 人 + give + 人 + 物
- Can you give me more food?
- Can you give us some beans
- Can you give her my letter?
- Duo will give me some potatoes tomorrow.
- Anna will give them some bananas.
- Give me the book. 

### give + 物 + to 人
- Give the book to me
- We are giving these books to them.
- Can you give the tickets to them.
- Will you give this book to them.(你会把这本书给他们吗？)
- Will you give that plant to your mom?(你会把那个植物给你的妈妈吗？)
- She wants to give movie tickets to her friend.

## get
### 買
- Do you want to get a tall bookcase or a short bookcase ?(你想要买一个高的书柜还是一个矮的书柜？)
- let's get some beans(我們去買一些豆子吧)
- Can we get tickets?(買機票)

### 抵達
- When will we get there?
- He'll get there at seven o'clock tonight.
- I want to go home, but when will I get there? (我想回家，但我什么时候会到家呢？)
- When she gets home, Priti reads a book in the garden.

### 拿東西
Can I get you a cup of tea.  
我可幫你拿一杯茶

### 找工作(取得工作)
You need to get a job.  
你需要找一份工作  

### 結婚
- when will Anna and Kevin get married?  
- My friend is getting married.

## show
- Emma wants to show us her new house.
- Can you show me your vegetable garden (你可以给我看看你的蔬菜园吗？)

## spend
### 人 + spend + 時間 + with 人
- Ben spent the weekend with his family 
- Ben spend time with his cat 

### 人 + spend + 時間 + 動作ing

- I spent three hours **cleaning** my room.
（我花了三個小時打掃房間。）
- She spends a lot of time **reading** books.
（她花很多時間閱讀書籍。）

### 人 + spend + 時間 + on + 東西

* I **spent** two hours **on** my homework.

東西 take 花時間
