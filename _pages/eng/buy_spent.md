## buy
### 人 + buy + 物

她下周会买一辆新车。  
She will buy a new car next week. 

### 人 + buy + 人 + 物

- She'll buy her parents some beans.
- Can you buy us some milk?

### 人 + buy + 物 + for + 人

通過努力(力氣)幫某人做某事 都要用for 人

ex:buy 買、make 做、cook 煮、get 拿

- I need to buy lots of fruit for a party.
- He wants to buy some plants for his flower garden(他想要给他的花园买一些植物。)

### 人 + buy + 物 + for + 錢
Can I buy it for one hundred dollars.
 
## pay
### 人 pay 錢 for 物
- You can pay for your drink[s] here.(你可以在這裡為你的飲料付錢，注意！此時飲料為可數名詞加s。)  
- I'm paying for the ride,

## get 買
- Do you want to get a tall bookcase or a short bookcase ?(你想要买一个高的书柜还是一个矮的书柜？)
- Can we get tickets?(買機票)


## spend
### 人 + spend + 時間 + with 人
- Ben spent the weekend with his family 
- Ben spend time with his cat 

### 人 + spend + 時間 + 動作ing

- I spent three hours **cleaning** my room.
（我花了三個小時打掃房間。）
- She spends a lot of time **reading** books.
（她花很多時間閱讀書籍。）

### 人 + spend + 時間 + on + 物

* I **spent** two hours **on** my homework.

## take
### 物 take 時間
making breakfast [takes a long time].  

## cost
### 物 cost 錢
- This blouse costs fifty dollars
