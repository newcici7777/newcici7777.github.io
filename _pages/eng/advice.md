## give/take advice
advice（建議）、help（幫助）、information（資訊） 這種**不可數**抽象名詞，前面沒有冠詞a，當你想表達「提供」給某人時，最常搭配的動詞就是 give。

advice 是一個不可數名詞，所以不能說 an advice 或 advices，要用`a piece of advice`

- She gave me **a piece of*** advice.（指給我一個建議）
- She gave me some advice.她給了我一些建議

- Will he take her advice? 他會接收她的建議嗎？(採納)
- take **a** suggestion

## advice on 名詞/Ving（關於的建議）
- If I need advice on getting a job, I'll ask you.如果我需要找工作的建議，我會問你。
- She gave me some great advice on writing a resume.
她給了我一些關於寫履歷的好建議。
- He asked his mentor for advice on career development.
他向導師徵求關於職涯發展的建議。

- I need some advice on choosing my college majors.
我需要一些關於選擇大學主修的建議。
- The teacher offered helpful advice on studying for the final exam.
老師提供了關於準備期末考的有益建議。
- Do you have any advice on learning a new language?
你有什麼關於學習新語言的建議嗎？

- She is looking for advice on managing her personal finances.
她正在尋找關於管理個人財務的建議。
- Can you give me some advice on buying a new laptop?
你能給我一些關於買新筆電的建議嗎？
- He took my advice on traveling to Japan.
他採納了我去日本旅遊的建議。
- They needed expert advice on health and nutrition.
他們需要關於健康與營養的專家建議。
