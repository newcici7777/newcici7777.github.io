date: 2026-08-24
keywords: english
---
- The hat you made is amazing!
  - 原本:The hat (that/which) was made by you is amazing!
  - 省略 關係代名詞(that/which) + be動詞 (was)
  - 簡化: The hat made by you is amazing.
  - 把by 刪掉，被動變主動(you變主詞)，是「你做的」，把you移到分詞前面，變成you made 

- The dress you bought is amazing! 「買衣服」這個動作已經在過去發生了！所以用過去式
- The book I read was sad.（我讀的那本書很悲傷。）
- The cake you baked is so delicious! （你烤的蛋糕超好吃！）
- The gift you gave me made my day. （你送我的禮物讓我一整天心情都很好。）
- The movie we watched was a bit boring. （我們昨晚看的那部電影有點無聊。）
- The book you recommended is incredible. （你推薦的那本書簡直太棒了。）
- The coffee I bought this morning is already cold. （我今天早上買的那杯咖啡已經冷掉了。）
- The photo you took of the sunset looks like a postcard. - （你拍的那張日落照片看起來像明信片一樣。）
- The song she sang at the party was very touching. （她在派對上唱的那首歌非常感人。）
- The restaurant we went to last night was amazing. （我們昨晚去的那家餐廳真的很棒。）
- The question you asked was actually quite difficult. （你剛剛問的那題其實蠻難的。）
- The jacket you left at my house is on the chair. （你遺留在我家、忘在椅子上的那件外套在這裡。）

- I love the movie we watched last night.（我喜歡我們昨晚看的電影)
