## How many times 頻率
以下有二種公式，單位a month放前面 或 放最後面。
```
How many times a month/week + 助動詞 + 主詞 + ... ?
How many times + 助動詞 + 主詞 + a month/week?
```

- How many times a month do you play golf? 一個月打幾次高爾夫球
- How many times do you play golf a month? 一個月打幾次高爾夫球

時間單位放前面:
- How many times a week do you work out? (你一個禮拜運動幾次？)
- How many times a day do you drink coffee? (你一天喝幾杯/幾次咖啡？)
- How many times a year do you go abroad? (你一年出國幾次？)

時間單位放後面:
- How many times do you go to the gym a week? (你一個禮拜去幾次健身房？)
- How many times do you check your emails a day? (你一天看幾次信箱？)
- How many times do you deploy code a month? (你一個月部署幾次程式碼？)

## How many times 現在完成式
詢問「總共去過幾次」（問經驗）
- How many times have you been to Japan? （你總共去過日本幾次？）
- How many times have you watched this movie? （這部電影你看了幾次？）
- How many times have you been to this café? (你來過這家咖啡廳幾次？)

### 回答
- Once. 一次（不說 one time）
- Twice. 兩次（不說 two times）
- Three times.(記得time加s)
- Many times.
- A few times.(只有幾次)
- Never.(從來沒有)
