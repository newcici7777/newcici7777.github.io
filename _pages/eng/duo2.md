## 進行式
- Bea is sitting on a bench in the park.
- Zari is babysitting her neighbor,
- Who are you writing to?
- I'm waiting for my friend.

## 身份
She's a teenager.

## really
excited用really,so修飾，不能使用very
- I'm really excited to meet
him. 
- It's so exciting!

## invite 人 to 目的地
- They are not going to invite us to the party(他们不打算邀请我们参加舞会。)
- Do we need to invite him to the party?
- I'm going to invite them to our house for dinner tonight.(我打算激请他们今晚来我家里吃晚饭。)
- I want to invite one hundred people to my party.

## meet 碰面
- they want to meet us at the theater.
- Do you want to meet at the big doors?
- Duo will meet them at the library
- Eddy meets his neighbor Vikram at the airport.
- tomorrow you will meet your new baby brother! 

## never
- We never work on Friday**s**
- First painting**s** are never good.
- we nerver finish the school early

## 建築物/房子/傢具 have 有
- Our school has a nice library.

## open close closed(形容詞)
### open 動詞
- That new restaurant opens tonight!(那家新餐厅今晚开业！)

### open 形容詞 closed 形容詞
- the book store is open.
- The museum [is open] [from] ten o'clock [to] five o'clock [on] Saturday, but it [is closed] [on] Sunday.

## 動詞 + into 動作感的進入
- Eddy walks into Oscar's apartment.
- Junior runs into his room.
- A janitor walks into the room.(janitor 為清潔員)

## carrying
- Eddy is carrying a painting.

## put
- I'm going to put these boxes in the living room. After lunch, can you help me put the refrigerator in the kitchen?
- Can you put this plant on the table?

## both A and B
- both Anna and I love our new house!
- Both Times Square and Wall street are in New York.

## both 二者
Both skirt[s] are green.

## each
each + 單數動詞
- each room has a TV!
- Each room has its key
- Each house has a bedroom.
- Is there a television in each bedroom?(在每个卧室里都有一台电视吗？)

## hard
- they work hard during the week.(hard作為副詞)
- this is a hard work. (hard作為形容詞)
- The test was hard yesterday.

## work
### 名詞work
- Eddy is [at work].

### 動詞
- I'm going to work. 上班

### work for 人/公司 (為誰工作)
- Is Anna working for two different companies ?
- Ben works [for] a good company.(本为一家很好的公司工作。)  

### work in 產業

## work + on + 日期 
I am not going to work on my birthday.  
I don't work on Sundays.

### work from home 在家工作
谁下周会在家工作？  
Who will [work from home] next week?  

### 下班
- What is Duo going to play [after work]?(多儿下班后打算玩什么？  )  
- Emma usually finishes work at half past eight.

## 上學 放學
- Anna and her boyfriend will be at the library after school.
- I won't be at school tomorrow.(我明天不会在学校。)
- My kids will finish school at ten past seven.(我的孩子们会在七点过十分放学。)
- When does your son finish school?

## all 代名詞
- I know all the roads in this citv.
- This phone knows all the roads in every city.
- And I want all my friends to come!
- We need to invite them all now!

## be + wearing 穿什麼衣服/穿什麼鞋子/戴什麼帽子
- Is she wearing a red hat ?

## kind of 種類
- This kind of cheese is nice!

## then 然後
- It rained then snowed.(下雨然後下雪)

## remember 
remember + to 
- I remembered to water your plants...

## only 只是
- I was only gone for a week!

## just 剛剛/僅僅
- what did you just eat?

## arrive at
- They arrive at Vikram's apartment.


## water
- I watered your plants three times every day!(澆水)
- We eat by the water.我们在水边吃。

## is visiting 短暫拜訪
Sorry Anna, I can't meet you. My friend [is visiting.]  

## its
- That sheep is eating its food right now
- That cow is eating its grass.
- This sheep has a big head, but its
eyes are very small.
- Is your watch in its box ?(你的手表在它的盒子里吗？)


## 無法分類
- That road **to** our village is long and busy.(到我们村子的那条路又长又繁忙。)
- Ben is angry at us
- She needs a bath
- France has beautiful mountains
