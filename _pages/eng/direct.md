## directions 實體路標或導航指示
- Are these the right directions?這些是正確的方向/指示嗎？
- Follow the directions.（照著導航/指示走）
  - Just follow the directions on Google Maps.
- Check the directions on my phone.(看我手機上的導航路線。）
- Look at the directions on the map.看地圖上的路線指示。）
- Wait, let me check the directions on my phone.
- I think my directions are wrong.
- Do you want to use my directions?
- The directions on my phone say that road is faster...
- Check the directions.(看一下導航指示)

## map
- Do you have a map of this city?
- Do you have a map of Shanghai?
- Check the map.查一下地圖

## in the north/east/south/west
- Is that city in the east or in the west?

## 移動動詞 + north/east/south/west
移動動詞: go, come, get, walk, drive, fly, move
中間沒有to，也沒有there，north/east/south/west 為副詞。

east、west、south、north 當作移動方向時，直接放在動詞後面（drive east, walk north）即可，非常簡潔好用！

- you need to drive east

## Trun left/right 
Trun left/right + at the ...

- Turn left at the next corner.（在下一個轉角向左轉。）
- The driver turns left and stops the car.
- Turn left at the end of the street

### trun for 距離 在多少距離後轉彎
- I don't need to turn for three more miles.(三英里後轉彎)

## Go left/right
- Go right at the traffic light.（在紅綠燈處往右轉/往右邊開。）

## Keep left / Keep right（交通標誌常見）

例如東西向的叫 Street，南北向的叫 Avenue。

## take a left/right 左轉右轉

## far away 很遠
far(遠) + away（離開、距離）

- The star is far away.（那顆星星好遠。）

## is far away from 離(什麼地方)很遠嗎？

be動詞 + far away + from + [基準地點]

from 從哪個基準點開始算起

- Do you live far away from the sea?
（你住的地方距離海邊很遠嗎？）
