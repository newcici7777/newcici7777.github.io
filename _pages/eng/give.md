有方向性的動詞，都用to
- send to you（寄給你  東西移動過去）
- pass to you（傳給你 方向移動）
- show to you（展示給你看  視線傳遞）
- write to you (寫信傳遞給你)

- give（給）  
  - Give the book to me.
  - Give me the book.
- send（寄/送 
  - Send a letter to him.
  - Send him a letter.
- pass（傳遞） 
  - Pass the salt to her.
  - Pass her the salt.
- show（展示/給看）  
  - Show the photo to us.
  - Show us the photo.
- tell（告訴） 
  - Tell the truth to them.
  - Tell them the truth.
- bring（帶來） 
  - Bring your homework to the teacher.
  - Bring the teacher your homework.
- write(寫信)
  - write a letter to me
  - write me a letter
- read
  - read a story to the child
  - read the child a story

|動詞|意思|範例 (Verb + 物 + to + 人)|
|:---------|:---------|:---------------------|
|hand|親手交給|She handed the letter to the cashier.（她把信親手交給收銀員。）|
|tell|告訴|Don't tell the secret to anyone.（別把秘密告訴任何人。）|
|teach|教導|He teaches English to kids.（他教小朋友英文。）|
|write|寫信給|I wrote a letter to my teacher.（我寫了一封信給老師。）|
|lend|借出|I lent my car to John.（我把車借給了約翰。）|
|offer|主動提供|They offered a job to her.（他們提供了一份工作給她。）|

  
## give
### 人 + give + 人 + 物
- I gave you a gift.
- Can you give me more food?
- Can you give us some beans
- Can you give her my letter?
- Duo will give me some potatoes tomorrow.
- Anna will give them some bananas.
- Give me the book. 

### give + 物 + to 人
- I gave a gift to you.
- Give the book to me
- We are giving these books to them.
- Can you give the tickets to them.
- Will you give this book to them.(你会把这本书给他们吗？)
- Will you give that plant to your mom?(你会把那个植物给你的妈妈吗？)
- She wants to give movie tickets to her friend.
- do you want my guitar? I can give it to you.

## send
### 人 + send + 人 + 物
- I want to send them some bananas from my farm.
- Grandpa will send us some new books.
- Can you send him an email?

### send + 人 + to 地方
- send him to a different office.

### 人 + send + 物 + to + 人


## show
### 人 show + 人 + 物
- Emma wants to show us her new house.
- Can you show me your vegetable garden (你可以给我看看你的蔬菜园吗？)
- Who showed you this place?谁给你看了这个地方？
- Zari's mother shows her three more blue sweaters.(Zari媽媽拿給她另外三件藍色毛衣／又給她看了三件藍色毛衣）

## lend to
```
lend + 某人 + 東西
lend + 東西 + to + 某人
```
- Can you lend me your pen?（你可以借我你的筆嗎？
- He lent some money to his friend.（他借了一些錢給他的朋友。）

## borrow from
```
borrow + 東西 + from + 某人
```
- Can I borrow your pen?（我能向你借你的筆嗎
- She borrowed a book from the library.（她從圖書館借了一本書。）


## offer + 人（受詞1） + 東西（受詞2）
- We can offer you a different room.
- We can offer a different room to you.
