注意在 third 之后，以下的所有数字都以
-th结尾。
one - first  
two - second  
three - third  
four - fourth  
five - fifth  
six - sixth   
seven - seventh   
eight - eighth  
nine - ninth  
ten - tenth  

另外，英语没有表示万的单词，你可以就说ten thousand

1,000 - one thousand  
10,000 - ten thousand  
100,000- one hundred thousand  

注意哦，跟中文不一样，逗号是每三位打一个，不是四位！
