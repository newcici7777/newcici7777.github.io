Say / Tell：可以直接接內容或子句（Say yes / Tell me you love me）。

## tell(及物)
tell是及物動詞，後面「一定要加人」。
除非tell 後面是一般名詞(不是動名詞/也不是名詞子句)，就可以不用加人。

### tell + 人(不可省略) + 內容(子句)
```
tell + 人 + 內容
```
- Can you tell the coach I'm sick?
- She told me I was handsome.
- Can you tell him that I will call him back? （你可以告訴他我會回電嗎？）
- He told his parents that he passed the exam. （他告訴父母他考試及格了——這裡省去了 that。）
- He told me (that) he passed the exam.（他告訴我他考試及格了。）
- She told me that she broke up with Eddy. （她告訴我她跟艾迪分手了 )

### Tell + 某人 + to V（叫某人去做某事）
- I told you to water the plants three times every week!
- My boss told me to finish the report by Friday. （我老闆叫我在星期五前完成報告。）
- She told the kids to wash their hands before dinner. （她叫孩子們晚餐前去洗手。）

### Tell + 某人 + Wh- 疑問子句
- Don't tell me what to do! （不要告訴我該怎麼做！／別對我指手畫腳！）
- He tells me how to do my job. 

### tell + 人 + 名詞
- Tell me something.
- I need to tell you something.
- He told me a secret.
- He told me very romantic things.
- He told me some things.
- Can you tell me more?(more 代名詞)

### tell + 名詞
後面接「特定的固定名詞（lies, a story, the truth, a joke...）」，直接當受詞即可，不需要加人：
- Don't tell lies.
- Can you tell a story?
- He told the truth.（他講了實話。）

------------------------
## say
轉述別人說過的話。

### say + 內容
- You said he's terrible!
- You said he is always late.
- She said (that) she was tired.（她說她很累。）
- She said she broke up with Eddy. （她說她跟艾迪分手了。）

### say 名詞 to 人(不可省略)
- He said very romantic things to me.
- He said hello to me.
- He said some things to me.

---------------

## `tell` vs. `say` 
這二個動詞都是說「內容」。條件限制如下:

### 內容
- tell + 人(不可省) + 內容
  - She told me I was handsome.
- say + 內容
  - You said he's terrible!

### 名詞
- tell + (人可省略) + 名詞
  - Don't tell lies.
- say + 名詞 + to + 人(不可省)
  - He said hello to me.

------------------------------

## talk(不及物) to/with/about 強調跟「誰」說話
talk 是一個不及物動詞（不能直接加受詞)  

talk 強調的是跟「誰」說話，而不是像tell/say著重在「內容」。  

如果要說內容，用tell或say。

### talk + to + 人
- you're talking to Leo!
- Talk to me.
- Talk to her!
- Can I talk to you?
- he decides to talk to her.
- We need to talk.(不及物)
- One day, he decides to talk to her.

### talk + with + 人
- He is talking with his friend.（他在跟他的朋友聊天。）
- Are you going to talk to her now?

### talk + about + 名詞
- What are they talking about?（他們在談論什麼？）
- Can we talk about Carl?
- What did he talk about?
- We don't need to go fishing to talk about things.

### talk + to + 人 + 名詞
- Talk to you about something.

-----------------------------------

## speak(不及物) to/with/about  強調跟「誰」說話
speak不及物（不能直接加受詞)  

speak「演講、發表談話」，跟上司說話、報告用speak  

### speak + to/with + 人
- May I speak to the manager?
- Speak to me.

### speak about + 主題
- He spoke about science.

### speak to 人 + about 主題
- He spoke to the students about science.(他向學生們發表了關於科學的演講。)

## speak(及物動詞)語言
speak + 語言
- She can speak English.（她會說英文。）

-------------------------------------


## talk/speak + about + Ving 動名詞片語
若talk/speak硬要說內容(子句)，要把動詞變成Ving，建議使用tell/say就好了。

### 主詞跟「內容的主詞」相同，省略內容主詞
- Mary talked about breaking up with Eddy. （瑪麗談到了她跟艾迪分手這件事。）
- Mary spoke about breaking up with Eddy. （瑪麗談論了關於她跟艾迪分手的事)

### 主詞不同，不能省略內容主詞，主詞要變受格(him,her,them)
- She talked about Mary breaking up with Eddy.
- They talked about the train being late.（他們談論火車誤點。）
- She talked about him being late.（她談到他遲到這件事。）
- She spoke about the team winning the game. （她談論了團隊贏得比賽這件事)

## talk/speak + about + wh-疑問詞子句(名詞)
除了Ving動名詞片語，也可以用wh-疑問
- They were talking about why the train was late.
- They talked about how the train was late.（他們談論火車是怎麼誤點的。）
