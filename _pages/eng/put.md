## put 放置
- I'm going to put these boxes in the living room. After lunch, can you help me put the refrigerator in the kitchen?
- Can you put this plant on the table?

## put... together ＝ 把...湊在一起、組合起來
- Now we can put it all together.現在我們可以把這一切整合在一起了。
- They put the flour, water, and salt together.他們把麵粉、水和鹽巴混和（放在）在一起。

## put gas in 加油
put 是「放、注入」，gas 是「汽油」，in 是「進去、裡面」。
- But I put gas in the car ten minutes ago. 

## put on makeup 把化妝品塗上去(正在進行的動作)
- wear makeup = 靜態，化妝品已戴在臉上的狀態。
- put on makeup = 動態，正在塗抹，正在化妝。

## put on 塗抹
```
put + [物品] + on + [表面/地方]
```
- Don't put too much butter on my bread.不要在我的麵包上放太多奶油。

## put on 放上去
- Please put some cheese on the pizza.（請在披薩上放一些起司。）
- He always puts cheese on his crackers.他总是在他的饼干上放奶酪。

## put on 穿上、戴上
在衣物配件的語境中（額外補充，意思不同）：當後面接衣服時，put on 就會變成「穿上、戴上」。
- Put on your jacket.（把你的外套穿上。）

## put a check 打勾勾
```
Put + 東西 + 地方 ＝ 把東西「放」在某處
```
- Put a check next to your answer.把一個「勾勾（check）」放（put）到答案的旁邊
- Put a cross in the box.（在格子裡打叉叉）
- Put your signature here.（把你的簽名放在這裡 → 請在此簽名）


## put down 寫下、記錄
把東西寫上去 (Put down)
- Let me put your phone number down. （讓我把你的電話號碼寫下來。）

## Put off（延期）
- We have to put off the meeting.（我們必須把會議延期。）


## put your hand up 舉手(單手)
- If you know the answer, put your hand up.你知道答案就舉手。
- 雙手: Put your hand**s** up!（把手舉起來！——警察抓犯人常用）

## Put on 播放
- Put on the movie.播放電影
- Put on music.（播放音樂）
  
### play music 放音樂
- He was playing music on his laptop.

### put on the movie 播放電影
- Can you put on the movie?（你可以播放電影嗎？）
- Let's put on a movie tonight.（我們今晚來看部電影吧！）
- Can you put on the movie we talked about yesterday? 你可以播放我們昨天聊的那部電影？
- Let's put on some cartoons for the kids to watch. 我們放一些卡通給小朋友看吧。
- He loves to put on late-night talk shows before going to sleep. 他喜歡在睡前看個深夜脫口秀。

### put on the news 播放新聞
- She put on the news to check the weather forecast. 她打開新聞來查看氣象預報。

### put on music 播放音樂
- Please put on some music.（請放一些音樂。）
- Please put on some relaxing music while we work. 我們工作時，請放一些輕鬆的音樂。
- Can you put on that song we heard in the car? 你可以放我們在車上聽的那首歌嗎？
- I like to put on jazz when I cook dinner. 我喜歡在煮晚餐時放爵士樂。


## put on the lights 燈打開
- It's getting dark, let's put on the lights.（天黑了，我們把燈打開吧。）
- It's getting dark outside, let's put on the living room lights. 外面天黑了，我們把客廳的燈打開吧。
- She put on the desk lamp so she could read clearly. 她打開檯燈，這樣她才能看清楚書。
- He put on the porch light to welcome his guests. 他打開門廊的燈來迎接客人。
