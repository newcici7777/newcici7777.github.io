## 現在式(習慣) 頻率副詞
always放在動詞前  
- Tom alwavs visits his grandma on her birthday. It's in May.Tom总是在他奶奶生日的时候去看望她。是在五月。
- Anna sometimes goes to work by car.
- Where does she usually play basketball?
- He often walks to school
- What do you usually draw?  
- Do you usually cook dinner?  
- l usually don't enjoy American food,but last night I really enjoyed it!


## 次數
```
次數 某個範圍單位
once a week 一周一次
two times a week 一周二次
three times every day 每天三次
three times every 2 day 每2天三次
three times every Sunday 每個禮拜天 三次
```
- I watered your plants three times every day!
- Three times every day?!
- I told you to water the plants three times every week!
- She practices guitar every Tuesday.(注意，guitar前面沒有the)  





Every day放句首  
Every day, he sees Lily drawing in the park.  

One day放句首
One day, he decides to talk to her.
